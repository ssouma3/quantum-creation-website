Two research approaches

The chapter distinguishes two approaches to connecting quantum-gate computation with music.

Wavefunction-based: uses the simulated quantum state directly for musical expression and quantum live coding.

Measurement-based: uses quantum-circuit measurement outcomes to generate musical events, including stochastic notes, chord-based schemes, phase modulation, and quantum-game-theoretic models.
