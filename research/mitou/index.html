IPA MITOU Target Project

Two quantum-gate-based approaches to music creation and performance, developed in the research initiated in 2018.

Project overview

The 2018 IPA MITOU Target research examined how quantum-gate operations could be connected with musical events and composition. The publication distinguishes a measurement-based approach from a wavefunction-based approach used for quantum live coding. The two methods use different forms of quantum information.

Research directions

Measurement-based Music Generation

Measurement outcomes of quantum circuits are mapped to discrete musical choices.
